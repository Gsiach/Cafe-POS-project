INCEPTION PHASE: The inception phase is the foundational task in the development of a software solution that focuses on defining the problem space clearly. It includes scope, problem statement, business case, high-level requirements and feasibility of a project. 

PROPOSED STRUCTURE

01_Project_Context: Merged introduction and business context, These are often very short. Combining them into a single 1_Project_Context.md creates a stronger opening that covers both the "Who" and the "Where."

02_Problem_Statement_and_Business_Case: Merged Problem_Statement and Business_Case. Links the "Pain Point" directly to the "Return on Investment." It's hard to justify a business case without the problem. A clear definition of the 'pain points' or gaps being addressed, the cost-benefit analysis and strategic value of solving the identified problem.

03_Vision_Scope_and_Stakeholders: Scope is often defined by the needs of the stakeholders. Having them in one place makes it easier to cross-reference user roles against the project boundaries.

04_High_Level_Requirements: Core features and functional/non-functional needs.

05_Risk_and_Feasibility: These go hand-in-hand. You evaluate if it's possible (Feasibility) while identifying what might go wrong (Risk).

08_Iteration_and_Project_Plan: Timeline of major milestones and detailed breakdown of the first few phases. Can include team structure and budget distribution.

07_Conclusion: Final thoughts on the project's viability. Includes the formal decision to proceed to the Elaboration/Construction phase(GO/NO-GO Recommendation).
