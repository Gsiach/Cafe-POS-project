1. Vision Statement

The vision of the Café Point of Sale (POS) System is to develop a modern, reliable, and intelligent digital platform that streamlines café operations, enhances customer experience, and improves business decision-making through real-time data and automation.

This system aims to replace inefficient manual processes with a secure, scalable, and user-friendly solution that empowers café owners, managers, and staff to operate efficiently, reduce errors, increase profitability, and deliver fast, high-quality service.

The long-term vision is to transform small and medium-sized cafés into digitally empowered businesses capable of competing in a modern technology-driven marketplace.

2. Project Scope

The Café POS System will provide a centralized platform to manage daily café operations including sales processing, inventory tracking, staff management, and reporting.

Core Functional Scope

The system will:

Allow cashiers and waiters to process customer orders for both counter (takeaway) and dine-in (table) service.

Automatically calculate totals, taxes, and generate digital receipts.

Manage inventory in real-time and update stock levels after each sale.

Alert management when stock reaches minimum threshold levels.

Provide secure login access for different user roles (Administrator, Manager, Cashier, Waiter, Kitchen Staff).

Generate daily, weekly, and monthly sales reports.

Track best-selling products and revenue trends.

Store transaction history securely in a database.

Support both cash and electronic payment recording.

Management & Analytics Scope

The system will:

Provide business intelligence dashboards for monitoring performance.

Analyze sales trends to assist in strategic decision-making.

Monitor employee activity and transaction logs.

Reduce financial leakages through accountability tracking.

Technical Scope

The system will:

Be built using a secure database-driven architecture.

Ensure data integrity and consistency.

Be scalable for future expansion (multiple branches).

Provide backup and recovery mechanisms.

Be user-friendly with an intuitive graphical interface.

3. Out of Scope (Limitations)

The following features are not included in the initial release:

Online ordering and delivery integration.

Mobile application version.

Integration with external banking APIs.

AI-based predictive inventory automation (future enhancement).

4. Expected Impact

The implementation of the Café POS System will:

Increase operational efficiency.

Reduce human error in sales recording.

Improve stock control and reduce losses.

Enhance customer satisfaction through faster service.

Provide data-driven insights for business growth.
